## Textbook

My Data Analysis courses at Central European University are built on the textbook
[*Data Analysis for Business, Economics, and Policy*](/textbook/) (with Gábor Kézdi),
with code in R, Python and Stata.

* Data Analysis 1 (chapters 01 to 06 of Békés-Kézdi) -- graduate level (applied MA/MS)
* Data Analysis 2 (chapters 07 to 12 of Békés-Kézdi) -- graduate level (applied MA/MS)
* Data Analysis 3 (chapters 13 to 18 of Békés-Kézdi) -- graduate level (MA/PHD)
* Data Analysis 4 (chapters 19 to 24 of Békés-Kézdi) -- graduate level (applied MA/MS)

* Applied Economics with Sports Data (Phd/Research MA)

* Data Analysis with AI 1 (BA/MA)
